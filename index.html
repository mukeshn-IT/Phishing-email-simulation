03 — Improve

Scores stay on this device (local storage). Do not use this exercise to send unsolicited messages.

      Prevention

      A short playbook for safer email habits

      These controls work at the person, process, and platform layers. Use them after the simulation, not instead of it.

      Recognize the pattern

      • Urgency, fear, or secrecy that blocks verification.
      • Lookalike domains (extra words, swapped letters, unusual TLDs).
      • Requests for credentials, MFA codes, wire transfers, or gift cards.
      • Unexpected attachments, especially executables or macro documents.
      • Display names that do not match the actual address.

      Verify before you act

      • Open the official site yourself. Do not follow the email link.
      • Confirm money or access requests on a known channel (callback to a listed number).
      • Hover to inspect destinations. Simulated links in Aegis show this skill.
      • Treat “the CEO said so” as a reason to slow down, not speed up.

      Harden the environment

      • Turn on phishing reporting in the mail client and MFA on accounts.
      • Keep a dual-control process for payments above a set amount.
      • Filter executable attachments at the gateway.
      • Run short, repeated awareness drills instead of one annual lecture.
